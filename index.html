00 / 导言

这段文字是什么What This Passage Is

这段德文是《红楼梦》卷首的转述，即通常所称的「作者自云」。曹雪芹在全书入口处借「作者」之口交代创作缘起 —— 历过一番梦幻，于是把真事隐去，借「通灵」之说写成《石头记》。在甲戌本等早期抄本中，这段文字位于卷首；学界一般认为它有「楔子」的性质，甚至可能出自脂砚斋批语所引述的作者自述。

需要留意的是，这段德文并非逐字翻译：它的末句 »Zhen Shiyin erkennt im Traum das beseelte Juwel«（甄士隐梦中识得通灵宝玉）对应的是第一回的回目「甄士隐梦幻识通灵，贾雨村风尘怀闺秀」，而原著正文只作「故曰『甄士隐』云云」。本页因此同时给出「原著回填」与「依德文直译」两个中文版本，供比对之用。

This German passage is a rendering of the opening of Honglou Meng — the lines conventionally known as “the author’s own words.” At the threshold of the novel, Cao Xueqin has the “author” account for his motive: having once passed through a dream, he conceals the true matter and, under the pretext of the tale of the spirit-endowed stone, writes the Story of the Stone. In early manuscripts such as the Jiaxu edition the passage stands at the head of the book; scholars generally treat it as a prologue (楔子), possibly quoting the author’s own statement as recorded in Zhiyanzhai’s commentary.

Note that the German is not a literal translation. Its final sentence — “Zhen Shiyin in his dream beholds the spirit-endowed jade” — corresponds to the couplet-title of Chapter 1, whereas the body of the novel reads only “hence the words Zhen Shiyin.” This page therefore offers two Chinese versions side by side: a restoration of the perceived original, and a literal rendering of the German.

03 / 通读

全文连读The Passage in Full

中文 · 原著回填

此开卷第一回也。作者自云：因曾历过一番梦幻之后，故将真事隐去，而借「通灵」之说，撰此《石头记》一书也。故曰「甄士隐」云云。

English · rendered from the German

The author speaks of himself: because he had once lived through a dream, he concealed the true event and wrote — under the pretext of the tale of the stone endowed with spirit (通靈 · tōnglíng, “the one who binds the spirits”) — this book, the Story of the Stone. Hence the words: “Zhen Shiyin in his dream beholds the spirit-endowed jade.”

此处英文系由德文转译，故保留德文末句的写法；若按中文原著直译，末句应作 “hence the words ‘Zhen Shiyin’ and no more.”

05 / 译注

译注Translator’s Notes

  1. 德文末句与原著有出入。原著作「故曰『甄士隐』云云」，德译者改写为 »Zhen Shiyin erkennt im Traum das beseelte Juwel«（甄士隐梦中识得通灵宝玉）—— 实际取的是第一回回目「甄士隐梦幻识通灵，贾雨村风尘怀闺秀」的说法。若做回译或版本比对，此处不宜当作文本直译。
    The final sentence departs from the source. The original reads only “hence the words ‘Zhen Shiyin’.” The German writer has substituted the wording of the Chapter 1 couplet-title. For back-translation or textual comparison, this line should not be treated as a literal rendering.
  2. 「通灵」的德语释义。德文把通靈解为 „die Geister Verbindende“（连接神灵者），是字面化的德语处理；中文语境中该词指「与神灵相通」，落到书中即那块灵石所具的灵性。英译取 “endowed with spirit” 以求兼顾字面与文意。
    “通靈” is glossed in German as die Geister Verbindende (“she who binds the spirits”) — a literalising move. In Chinese the term means “in communion with the spirits,” and in the novel it denotes the numinous quality of the stone itself.
  3. 「甄士隐」为谐音。谐「真事隐」（真事隐去），与「贾雨村」谐「假语村言」相对，是全书「真／假」结构的入口。翻译时通常保留音译，不作释义。
    “甄士隐” is a homophone of 真事隐, “the true matter concealed,” set against “贾雨村” = 假语村言, “fictitious words in rustic speech.” Together they open the novel’s true/false architecture. Translators conventionally keep the name transliterated without glossing.
